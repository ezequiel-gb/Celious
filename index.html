    YOUR CONNECTION, ON YOUR TERMS

    Celious Switch.

    A real Windows tray application controls the connection between your local Celious service and ChatGPT. Turn it on when you want access, and off when you do not. It is part of the installation, not a browser demonstration.

    On connects. Off disconnects.

    The connection runs from your computer. Phone access requires that computer and its connection to remain online. Startup begins off; exiting clears the session credential.
